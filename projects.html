AI-Based Assistant Chatbot for Farmers

Designed and implemented an intelligent chatbot that assists farmers with crop selection, fertilizer recommendations, pest management, weather updates, and government schemes. Integrated NLP for regional language support, KNN-based machine learning for crop recommendation, and voice input/output features to improve accessibility for rural users.

Visitor System

Developed a comprehensive website for managing visitor requests within the organization. The system allows visitors to submit their visiting requests online via a form. It then processes these requests and sends automated email notifications to inform visitors whether their requests have been accepted or rejected by the company. The website features a user-friendly interface, secure data handling, and an efficient backend system to streamline the visitor management process.

Plagiarism Checker

The Plagiarism Checker is a software tool designed to detect and highlight copied or duplicated content in academic, professional, or online texts. It compares user-submitted content against a large database of documents, websites, and publications to identify similarities and ensure content originality. The goal is to promote academic integrity and content authenticity.

AI Legal Assistant

The AI Legal Assistant is an intelligent system designed to assist legal professionals, students, or the general public by providing quick, accurate, and context-aware legal support. The system utilizes Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Machine Learning (ML) techniques to interpret legal queries, retrieve relevant legal documents, summarize case laws, and even generate legal drafts such as notices, contracts, or affidavits.
